# Crust & Bloom

A personal blog about specialty coffee and sourdough bread.

The coffee side covers drinks I make at home: cappuccinos, lattes, and filter coffee, with notes on beans, ratios, and technique. The bread side covers my sourdough baking, from plain country loaves to inclusions like parmesan-rosemary, fig-walnut, and dark chocolate-cherry.

Everything here is from my own kitchen, with real recipes and real photos.
