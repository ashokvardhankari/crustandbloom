# Crust & Bloom

A personal blog about two things I can't stop thinking about: specialty coffee and sourdough bread.

The coffee side covers the drinks I make at home — cappuccinos, lattes, and filter coffee — with notes on beans, ratios, and technique. The bread side covers my sourdough baking, from plain country loaves to inclusions like parmesan-rosemary, fig-walnut, and dark chocolate-cherry.

Everything here is from my own kitchen. Real recipes, real photos, and the kind of detail I wished I'd found when I was first learning.
